## Review and adoption

The gallery contains 91 preserved PNGs: 38 current selections and 53 alternatives, intermediate studies or earlier selections. Six original images retain direct user approvals. Other choices use delegated judgment and remain individually draft in the record.

Native pixels and alpha are preserved. A few cutouts need edge cleanup; design sheets do not prove mechanical connections or identical geometry. Complete animation skins, 3D models, functional UI and gameplay-scale Godot review remain separate production work.

## City-square rethink — 2026-10-29

The user requested a denser, larger city centre directly adjoining the workshop and a bigger power landmark. Four new imagegen alternatives, A–D, are [available for selection](../../../../art/source/world/sparks-square/v2-city-concepts/README.md). Exact prompts and original output/reference hashes accompany the images; no option is selected yet. The earlier concept pack is preserved.

The follow-up [E — Guildhall Square](../../../../art/source/world/sparks-square/v3-guildhall-square/README.md) combines D’s square and central beacon with C’s guild hall on the main ground level. The user requested less verticality, more openness and room for later world building; the original alternatives remain preserved.
