# Purge of Kindle Hollow and Village Morning — 2026-11-05

At the user's request the game was reduced to **Sparks & Machines** alone. Everything below is gone from the working tree; all of it is recoverable (see the end).

## Removed

- **Campaigns:** `kindle-hollow`, `village-morning` and their level entries; areas `kindle-hollow`, `morning-bedroom`, `morning-town-hall-room`, `village-morning`, `village-cottage`, `village-workshop`, `village-loft`, `village-mill`, `rune-mill`; the archived `kindle-hollow-v1` document; `content/villagers.json`, `content/interiors/catalog.json` and the fairy's `content/companions/hotaru.json`.
- **Models (91 catalog entries, Blender sources and runtime GLBs):** the six cottages, cabins and mills (`cottage`, `concept-cottage`, `reference-cottage`, `painted-cottage`, `log-cabin*`, `mill-house`, `brook-mill`, `hearth-house`, `lantern-loft`, `hollow-bakery`, `hollow-smithy`), the village trees and scenery (`tree`, `tree-stump`, `hearth-oak`, `ridge-cedar`, `river-willow`, `painted-oak`, `rootward-tree`), the clearing's ground and lamppost, the 43 `hollow-*` builder-library furnishings and 13 other props, `morning-town-hall`, and Sparks-era models that earlier square layouts had superseded (`square-*`, `guildhall-square-set`, `haverlund-square-set`, `loft-tape-machine`, `loft-cable`, `loft-hand-terminal`). Also removed: the authoring-record folders `building-polish`, `doodads-kit`, `doorways`, `expansion-kit`, `illustrated-village`, `village-landmarks` and `village-library`. The Rootwell concept paintings that lived in `square-beacon/concept-v1/` were kept.
- **Art:** the fairy Hotaru's runtime sprite and portrait, the old village cast portraits (Mira, Rowan, Bram, Tessa; Nell's is still the loft's), the village dialogue concept sheets. **Sprite sheets and skins are kept** (`art/source/characters/`, `assets/characters/`, the fairy's source under `art/source/companions/hotaru/`).
- **Code:** the Kindle notebook (`NotebookPanel`, `LessonView`, `JournalPanel`, reference/world views), Village Morning's movement-key and greeting lessons with their rules (`MovementBindings`, `world.controls`), the village conversation and dialogue rules, the epilogue and level-select screens, the fairy actor, the first-person builder "Write code for it" hook, the old residents' marks and talk prompts.
- **Tests and tools:** sixteen campaign tests (`morning`, `places`, `landmarks`, `house_flow`, `builder_library`, `code_build`, `prop_flow`, `material_flow`, `dialogue`, `build_mode`, ...; the builder test became `test_layout_flow` over the square) and 28 review and tour tools; `scripts/screenshot.sh`.
- **Documents:** `docs/concept/v0.6`, the build-mode and ground sub-tile handoffs, `pipeline/{MORNING,BUILDINGS,BUILDER_LIBRARY,DIALOGUE}.md`, 23 pipeline reviews, concept variants, the unselected "future" plates and gallery files of v0.7, and the campaigns' evidence captures.
- **Asset requests:** the 16 open deletion requests for models that are now gone were removed with them (the 30 earlier deletion receipts remain). Finished requests for the clockshop and square ground now carry stale fingerprints because those masters were revised the same day; they need re-review.
- **Builder shelf:** `content/build/catalog.json` now offers only models that ship (34 entries; the square's layout studio uses fourteen of them).

## Kept on purpose

- The **build engine** (`core/area_build.gd`, `core/build_ledger.gd`, `app/build_mode.gd`, their UI): the square's layout studio runs on it. Houses, procedural trees, terrain and water tools, room finishes and instanced house rooms are still in that engine without any shipped content. Their rule tests run over `test/fixtures/` (the removed village and clearing as plain JSON with footprint-only model entries); `AreaStore.use_fixtures()` points the stores at them. Remove those features, and the fixtures, together when the studio's future is decided.
- **Save filenames:** Sparks still saves to `kindle_hollow.save.sparks-and-machines.json` in the `RootwardVoxelLab` user folder, so existing saves load. Old Kindle Hollow and Village Morning saves in that folder are no longer read and can be deleted.
- Older Sparks asset versions (`v1`, `v2`, ... masters and their records) and the Sparks concept record in `docs/concept/v0.7`–`v0.8`.

## Getting anything back

Everything tracked was removed from the working tree only; nothing was committed or rewritten. `git checkout HEAD -- <path>` restores tracked files (HEAD at the time was `fa4fa62`). Files that were modified or untracked on 2026-11-05 are in `~/Voxel-Rootward-pre-purge-delta-2026-11-05/working-tree-delta.tar.gz` (list in `files.nul`, status in `git-status.txt`). Delete that folder once you are satisfied.
