# Review: village expansion, 2026-10-23–24

- Area/source versions: Kindle Hollow v2, four village room templates v1, Rowan/Bram/Tessa/Nell shared-frame sources v1. The old area document is archived; the approved boy, Mira and the shared character template are preserved.
- Intended change: make the valley larger while keeping its camera/style and programming quests; let houses open separate saved rooms; add four resident identities alongside Mira; hide the starting terrain underside and enrich the river.
- Implemented: 96×80 cells versus 64×48 (2.5 times the area), 21 buildings, 224 playable trees, fourteen zones, five residents, all six expansion models on the builder shelf and in the new districts. Each registered house asset opens a cottage/workshop/loft/mill instance addressed by area/building id. Rooms keep separate lamp/discovery/memory state, Continue resumes indoors, and exit restores the valley and doorstep. New house ids prevent room reuse after removal or undo.
- Art build actually run: guarded source assembly, existing integer-registration packer for each new skin, then `VOXEL_LAB_FRESH=1 scripts/art.sh`. Seven registered skins compiled, including the hidden tint example. Source prompts, original generation outputs, exact mirrors, eye patches and hashes are preserved with each skin; model/seed were not exposed by the image tool. See [source notes](../../art/source/characters/village-v1.md).
- Gameplay resolution/camera/renderer: windowed 1600×1000, Godot 4.7.2 GL Compatibility on AMD Radeon 680M. Outdoor zoom 13 and original heading/elevation retained; rooms use zoom 11 and the following camera. The complete final tour measured about 167–168 FPS; an earlier run under different desktop load measured 78–102 FPS. These are local short samples, not a release hardware benchmark.
- Actual windowed checks: `tools/village_tour.gd` captured the start boundary, new districts, flowing river, all resident conversations, cottage/workshop/loft/mill rooms, Mira at home and both shelf categories. It uses scratch saves. A second complete tour at 1280×720 passed too, including walking around furniture to the far corner of all four room types; the boy stayed visible. Indoor Pause, Character and room-only Reset screens were additionally reviewed at 1280×720 with the `menus` mode. `tools/villager_character_review.gd` rendered 24 actors: all four shared walks per identity, then neutral breathing and twelve front blinks; rear views stayed open.
- Quest playthrough actually run: `VOXEL_LAB_FRESH=1 scripts/play.sh --resolution 1600x1000 -s res://tools/playthrough.gd -- .cache/village-expansion/playthrough`. The real notebook Run/Apply flow completed **5 of 5 quests, 19 lamps lit, failed=false**.
- Final automated result: `scripts/test.sh` exited 0 with **2,022 checks passed across all 17 suites**. This includes 93 places/village checks, 22 actual house-travel/save/reset checks, all five Python/WASI quest solutions, 66 shared-frame checks, 271 world-art checks and 497 handoff checks. No script/parse errors were found in the final import, test or windowed-review logs. Logs remain disposable under `.cache/village-expansion/`.
- In-game observations and fixes: fresh light pools replace old scene references during travel; cloth covers and rugs use woven materials; resident positions inside homes come from room documents; the following camera keeps the boy visible away from the entry. Resident motion pauses during dialogue; the boy remains the player in Character and on Continue. Visits to multiple houses, room state isolation, indoor Continue, old saves without room data, owner presentation, room-only Reset and a builder-created house are covered by actual scene tests. The Character screen keeps the overall journey statistics while indoors.
- Layout audit: all original buildings, trees, lesson mechanisms, spawn, gates and bridge were retained. Thirty existing walkable cells at the north/east edges became connecting dirt roads; the original river and quest locks were preserved.
- Source audit: boy runtime pixels, Mira source/runtime pixels, shared template and selected foundation Blender/runtime files retain their recorded hashes. Two separate prop source files (grain-sacks and woodpile) changed during concurrent authoring; this pass did not modify or restore them. No adopted model was regenerated for this change.
- Limitations: rooms are furnished procedural cutaways on one logical plane, not generated floor plans or editable furniture. Residents have short deterministic strolls and static dialogue, not daily schedules or quests. The same resident can be presented locally indoors and outdoors; there is no global NPC simulation. Brook Mill's separate static wheel remains decorative. Water is a visual surface and spray, not fluid simulation. General travel between outdoor regions remains future work.
- Decision: implemented and reviewed as a playable expansion; user acceptance of the new content remains a separate visual decision. No commit, release or publication was created.
