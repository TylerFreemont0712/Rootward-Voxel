# Modular background houses D–H — 2026-10-29

The user wants the town dressed from finished, reusable houses rather than one large set model, and asked for two of the [v0.72 background-house concepts](../../docs/concept/v0.72/background-houses.md), detailed, using the new [seaside texture pack](../../docs/concept/v0.72/house-textures.md), with minor per-house tweaks so a street of them is not samey. Not placed in the game; reviewable in the title's Asset Showcase (Dev). Mid-pass, the user asked that the paving apron (the sidewalk) not be part of the house; it was removed. After seeing E and F, the user asked for the other three (D, G, H) from the same kit.

## Result

- **Five houses**, new editable Blender masters with audited GLBs, catalogued `showcase_only`: `seaside-lantern-row-house` (D, tall and narrow, street gable), `seaside-saltwind-house` (E, broad, hipped), `seaside-rosewash-house` (F, compact, jettied), `seaside-shingle-court-cottage` (G, low and wide, four dormers, optional courtyard gate) and `seaside-eaveside-terrace` (H, two joined homes with a stepped roofline). Source pack and conventions: [seaside houses README](../../art/source/world/seaside-houses/v1/README.md). E and F came first to prove the kit on both roof types; D, G and H then needed only three kit additions.
- **Shared kit** (`house_kit.py`): textured walls with lined openings, quoins, string courses, surrounds, casement windows with curtains and braced shutters, plank/panelled/arched doors and hoods, stepped pan-tile roofs (gable, hip, lean-to, dormer) whose painted tile band matches each geometric course, eave mouths, ridge/hip/verge caps, gutters, downpipes, chimneys, lanterns and yard dressing. For D–H it gained a turned authoring frame (street-facing gables reuse the ridge-along-X roof), `_2` surface slots so a joined building's second home paints independently, an enclosed lean-to shed and a bench, and material parameters on its roof, dormer, window, door and hood helpers.
- **Per-placement variety** (`art/house_variants.gd`): six painted surface families with three colours each (H: four more for its east home, left out of the curated palettes so the pair stays two-tone), three curated palettes from the texture library, optional dressing (window boxes, planters, vines, yard, log store, laundry) and alternatives (brick or stone chimney, open or drawn shutters, Saltwind's oak or painted front door). A seed gives a deterministic look. Instances share the imported materials; swaps are per-instance overrides cached by painting.
- **Showcase**: a house shows a **Look** menu (Authored, Cool harbour, Ochre court, Warm lane, Shuffled), **Shuffle**, **Street of three** (two shuffled neighbours either side) and a one-line summary of the look on show. Other models are unchanged.
- **Texture build**: the seaside library sets `detail_size: 512`; `scripts/art.sh` now also writes `assets/world/textures/seaside-houses/detail/` (15 lossless mipmapped maps) and copies the palette presets into the manifest. Every existing runtime file kept its exact bytes (hash audit of all 1,105 files under `assets/` before and after).
- No paving or sidewalk: each house stands on the ground it is placed on. Rear dressing (porch, alcove, log store, washing pole) stays within about 2.2 m of the back wall.

## Not done

- Not placed in any area, and area `buildings` entries cannot yet carry a variant (the hook is `WorldAssets.instantiate(id, cache, variant)`). No collision rectangles beyond the declared `logical_footprint`; doors are not enterable.
- Variety is limited to paintings, dressing and alternatives. Dimensions, storeys and mirrored plans are fixed per model.
- Weight (hidden alternatives included): Lantern Row 43,406 triangles / 26 surfaces, Saltwind 48,610 / 36, Rosewash 36,126 / 28, Shingle Court 33,992 / 30, Eaveside pair 64,712 / 32. Plain colours share one palette material, which cut E and F from 83 and 91 surfaces with no visible change. Illustrated outlines roughly double the drawn triangles. A full town has not been measured on the user's laptop.
- Shingle Court's optional gate stands 2.3 m east of its walls, outside `logical_footprint`; a placement next to it must leave room or hide the gate.
