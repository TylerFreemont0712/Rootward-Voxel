## Movement

`core/village_life.gd` owns movement at 60 Hz, with ordinary `LabWorld` passage checks. Sprites and robots reserve both cells of a step, route around each other and the player, wait when blocked, and pause at their route stops. Doors, walk-through arrivals, spawn cells, zone-screen approaches and lamp code stands are reserved for player access. An intermediate step continues directly into the next one so sprites keep their walk phase instead of briefly showing neutral at each cell.

`scenes/villager_view.gd` presents existing painted sprites and existing placed robot models. Wheels turn from actual distance and authored wheel radius, adjusted for model scale. Heading turns smoothly and feet/wheels follow the existing walking surface. Explicit `skin` or `object` resident definitions have no dialogue identity, and only speaking identities can create conversation markers. Their IDs never select the archived village's dialogue.

Entry/Continue restarts local ambient routes. A resident whose start coincides with the saved player moves to the nearest connected free cell; the player's save is unchanged. Notebook, modal, title and build mode pause the simulation. The old presentation-only `interior.life.robots` remains supported for archived area documents; active square robots each have one logical movement owner.

The review records movement in each populated area, with fourteen 400 ms samples per viewpoint. Both runs observed all cast, townspeople and robots moving. The final 1280 review includes the continuous-walking correction. An actual player step into Pip's reserved cell was refused. Nell reads clearly beside the approved boy; the small workshop Tender stays grounded, and the lane's Pip/Wick/Trowel remain legible against the street.
