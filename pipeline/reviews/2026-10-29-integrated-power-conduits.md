# Integrated power conduits — 2026-10-29

The earlier flat-grid pass has been replaced in the adopted square, lane and lane-surround Blender masters. Conduits now have recessed paving, bronze frames, inset illuminated panes and branch service plates. Their surfaces follow the actual modeled steps, including risers and beveled nosings. The lane trunk runs through the center of the town gate and tram stairs; branches cross the houses' threshold steps and feed the tram shelter. The platform has a lengthwise supply. The route continues down the modeled town steps beyond the lane and along the square's exit streets past its playable boundary.

Separate wall stripes were removed. Existing retaining-wall pilasters keep their exact shapes and receive a stone-based carved emission pattern: 38 in the square and 17 around the lane. The original Rootwell ring remains ordinary brass. Glow is confined to the inset glass and pillar carving. `art/illustrated_edges.gd` keeps expanded outline hulls off the already-framed inlays so they cannot cover the recessed glass.
