# Sparks workshop source pack

The downstairs room follows the selected [T7b workshop](../../../../../docs/concept/v0.7/art/4-opening/T7b-workshop-floor.png) and [P3 props](../../../../../docs/concept/v0.7/art/4-opening/P3-workshop-props.png). Twenty-one new editable Blender masters provide the shell and herringbone floor, full staircase, repair bench, tool wall, parts drawers, assembly table, parts crate, stove and kettle, armchair and throw, tea table, job board, shop doors and flowers, pendant lamps, stools, rugs, wall shelves, umbrella crock, bread cupboard, service trolley, clock and addressable stair lantern. Twenty-seven placements include reuse of the loft's approved tape reader, Hotaru and harbour picture.

`author.py` records guarded one-time authoring of the first seventeen models; `household_additions.py` does the same for the three household additions. Both refuse an existing master. Deliberate source edits preserve before copies and hashes: `review_sources.py` separates the window/tools and supports the porch; `textile_finish.py` and `rug_surface_finish.py` finish the woven surfaces; `touchup_review.py` removes all three white covers, opens the crock, raises the flowerpots onto the step, supports the chair throw and cushion, joins the stove bars and extends its flue for the smaller stove. `interior_floor_finish.py` faces the crock's recessed bottom into the opening; `cushion_clearance_finish.py` moves the loose cushion clear of the throw's rear fold. The read-only `validate_geometry.py` checks the chair intersections and pot/crock support. Future work starts by opening the current `model.blend`, never by regenerating it. Normal export: `scripts/world-art.sh build <asset-id>`.

New native 1536×1024 paintings are [workbench oak](workbench-oak.png) and [plaid wool](wool-plaid.png), created with the built-in imagegen tool. [Exact prompts and hashes](prompts.json) record both outputs and the four unchanged loft paintings: oak, plaster, harbour and woven rug. All selected images are packed into the appropriate editable masters and embedded in the runtime GLBs. No generator or Blender process is needed during play.

The world catalog owns source/runtime selection and outlines. `content/areas/workshop-floor.json` owns placements, blocking furniture, inspections, stair heights and the reciprocal connection to the loft. Its furniture instances are 15% smaller, with coordinated tabletop props, collisions and mounting positions; the traveller, stairs, room and door stay at their approved scale. A food cupboard holds an open labelled tin, scored loaf, slices, knife and crumbs beneath a clock. A caster trolley carries parts, oil and reference books. Static source geometry never decides traversal. The street doors are latched; the job board, bread cupboard and repair corners can be inspected. Nell's arrival, street travel and outdoor jobs remain future work.

The journey stores each floor's objects, position and program memory separately. Its separate notebook drafts and greeting progress are shared. Continue can resume downstairs, and old loft-only saves remain valid. The earlier loft sources and paintings are preserved.

The [furnished-room gameplay review](../../../../../pipeline/reviews/2026-10-27-workshop-floor.md) links native 1600×1000 and 1280×720 captures. `validation.json` records source/runtime hashes, preserved masters, painting hashes, rebuild evidence and checks. `validate_review.py` records the furnished-room milestone. The current [first-jobs review](../../../../../pipeline/reviews/2026-10-27-workshop-first-jobs.md), `story-validation.json` and `validate_story.py` cover the later stair lantern, lamp fixture bindings, scene/quest chain and windowed story checks. The new lantern is first-authored by the guarded `stair_lamp.py`; the other masters retain their edits.

The [tablet and wall screen review](../../../../../pipeline/reviews/2026-10-27-tablet-and-wall-screen.md) adds `workshop-wall-screen` (guarded first authoring in `wall_screen.py`, the zone node the field tablet jacks into) and revises two masters once through `tool_wall_and_mug.py`: the tool wall’s looped hangers become a shadow board with a four-slot spanner rack (the third spanner is the one on the bench), a claw hammer on its pegs and two screwdrivers, and the tea table’s mug becomes hollow. Prior masters are kept as `model.pre-tool-wall-and-mug.blend`; `tool-wall-and-mug.json` records their hashes.

The [loft and stairs review](../../../../../pipeline/reviews/2026-10-28-loft-and-stairs.md) turns the staircase round: the area document places the unchanged `workshop-staircase` master turned 180° in its own footprint, so it climbs from the north wall to the middle of the room. The north wall is the party wall with Nell's house on Spindle Lane, so `party_wall_revision.py` deliberately removes only its window, sill and pot from the adopted shell (prior master `model.pre-party-wall.blend`; `workshop-shell/v1/party-wall-revision.json` records the hashes and the removed pieces), and the guarded `jar_shelf.py` first-authors `workshop-jar-shelf` to hang in its place.
