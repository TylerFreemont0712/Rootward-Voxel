# Asset brief: woodpile

Status: candidate. On the studio build shelf's Doodads tab (`content/build/catalog.json`, kind `prop`) and registered in `pipeline/world-catalog.json`; not placed in any shipped area.

- Purpose and player-visible role: decorative farmyard prop, a stacked woodpile by a cottage or mill. It is not placed in any shipped area; the studio can place it from the build shelf's Doodads tab.
- Reference and the three visual qualities to match: round split logs with visible rings at their ends, bark shaded by side, and a neat two-row stack in flat voxel colours.
- Source folder/version and authoring tool/version: `art/source/world/woodpile/v1/model.blend`, Blender 5.2.2 LTS, generated by `art/source/world/doodads-kit/v1/farmyard.py` (re-authored once after review: `-- woodpile`).
- Exact prompt or generator/export steps: `blender --background --factory-startup --python art/source/world/doodads-kit/v1/farmyard.py -- woodpile` (only after removing this source), then `scripts/world-art.sh build woodpile`.
- Dimensions in world units and comparison to player: about 0.9 m long, 0.4 m deep and 0.45 m tall. Logs are 0.25 m across. Voxels are 0.05 m.
- Ground pivot and forward direction: ground origin at the centre of the stack. The log ends face -Y in Blender, which is +Z in Godot.
- Visible overhang versus solid logical footprint: the proposed footprint is the stack, `[-0.45, -0.2, 0.9, 0.4]`. Nothing overhangs it.
- Shared materials, textures and import requirements: flat colours only. Bark uses `oak`, `oak_light`, `wood`, `wood_light` and `wood_dark`. Cut ends use `cut` and `cut_ring`, both defined in `farmyard.py` as pale cut wood, not in the shared palette.
- Variant names and parts that move or change state: none. Objects are grouped by the `part` property: `Logs` and `Timber`.
- Fields/commands exposed to player programs, or decorative only: decorative only.
- Active runtime output and catalog entry: `assets/world/woodpile/model.glb` and its `manifest.json`; catalog id `woodpile`, authority `blender`, status `candidate_unplaced`.
- Build command and second-placement/rebuild check: `scripts/world-art.sh build woodpile`. Placed through build mode in `test/test_build_mode.gd` and `tools/doodad_tour.gd`, which also exercise move, delete and save.
- Acceptance review/evidence and known limitations: at gameplay zoom the round ends read as split logs and the stack is clearly a woodpile, which is the improvement over the square-section version. The upper row sits in the gaps, so it reads as a stack rather than a loose pile.
