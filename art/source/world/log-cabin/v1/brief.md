# Asset brief: log-cabin

Status: accepted as the first concept-matched house (adopted 2026-10-23); see [the review](../../../../../pipeline/reviews/2026-10-23-log-cabin-and-lamppost.md).

- Purpose and player-visible role: a home for Kindle Hollow's villagers, a less generic alternative to the `cottage`. Placed as `home`, `row-north-a` and `row-south-a`. Decorative; it is not enterable.
- Reference and the three visual qualities to match: the violet-roofed cabin at the upper left of [reference/voxel-isometric.png](../../../../../reference/voxel-isometric.png). (1) Saturated violet roof of chunky tiles, each with a lighter lip, and a stone chimney. (2) Horizontal log walls with dark chinking between the logs and round log ends under the gable edges, on a pale limestone foundation. (3) An arched stone door surround, a round window in the gable and a warm glowing side window.
- Source folder/version and authoring tool/version: `art/source/world/log-cabin/v1/model.blend`, Blender 5.2.2 LTS. First geometry came from the one-time recipe `cabin()` in `pipeline/world_recipes.gd`, seeded to `seed.glb` and imported by `init`. The `.blend` is the master from then on.
- Exact prompt or generator/export steps: `scripts/world-art.sh seed log-cabin`, `init`, `build`, `compare` (all passed; the round trip is lossless). Design iteration happened before `seed`, with `tools/asset_shot.gd -- log-cabin <png> recipe`.
- Dimensions in world units and comparison to player: foundation 2.90 × 2.86; log walls 2.72 × 2.68; eave 2.2 high, ridge about 3.6, chimney top 4.67; roof 3.55 wide. The door is 0.74 wide and its stone arch tops out at about 1.64, against a character about 1.3 tall.
- Ground pivot and forward direction: ground origin at the centre of the footprint. The door, the doorstep and the round gable window face +Z (area-document facing `S`); `E` turns the door toward +x. A smaller round window and a plain gable are at the back.
- Visible overhang versus solid logical footprint: the catalog footprint `[-1.45, -1.43, 2.9, 2.86]` is the foundation. It blocks exactly the same three-by-three cells as the cottage at a whole-cell placement (`test/test_area.gd` proves it). The roof overhang (0.28 along each slope, 0.26 past each gable), the doorstep (out to z = 1.83) and the chimney are decoration, not blocking.
- Shared materials, textures and import requirements: the shared palette only: `oak`, `oak_light`, `wood`, `wood_light`, `wood_dark`, `roof`, `roof_light`, `roof_dark`, `stone_warm` (new), `stone_light`, `stone_dark`, `window`, `window_glow` (new, emissive), `iron`, `gold`. 64×64 mipmapped textures are packed in the `.blend` and exported in the GLB. Logs are turned a quarter so the grain runs along them.
- Variant names and parts that move or change state: none. Nothing moves; the side windows always glow.
- Fields/commands exposed to player programs, or decorative only: decorative only.
- Active runtime output and catalog entry: `assets/world/log-cabin/model.glb`, catalog id `log-cabin`, authority `blender`.
- Build command and second-placement/rebuild check: `scripts/world-art.sh build log-cabin`. Three placements in Kindle Hollow, two of them facing differently (`S`, `E`).
- Acceptance review/evidence and known limitations: [evidence/cabin-lamppost-vs-concept.png](../../../../../evidence/cabin-lamppost-vs-concept.png) and [evidence/hollow-cabin-lamppost.png](../../../../../evidence/hollow-cabin-lamppost.png). Limits: tiles are boxes, so there is no rolled tile edge as in the concept; the round window and arch are stepped from boxes; there is no porch, flower box, barrel or bench (excluded props).
