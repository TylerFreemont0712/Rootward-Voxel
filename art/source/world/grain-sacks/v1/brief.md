# Asset brief: grain-sacks

Status: candidate. On the studio build shelf's Doodads tab (`content/build/catalog.json`, kind `prop`) and registered in `pipeline/world-catalog.json`; not placed in any shipped area.

- Purpose and player-visible role: decorative farmyard prop, a pile of grain sacks by a barn or mill. It is not placed in any shipped area; the studio can place it from the build shelf's Doodads tab.
- Reference and the three visual qualities to match: chunky voxel forms in flat colours, a warm burlap tone that is clearly not limestone, and a gathered twine tie at each mouth.
- Source folder/version and authoring tool/version: `art/source/world/grain-sacks/v1/model.blend`, Blender 5.2.2 LTS, generated by `art/source/world/doodads-kit/v1/farmyard.py` (re-authored once after review: `-- grain-sacks`).
- Exact prompt or generator/export steps: `blender --background --factory-startup --python art/source/world/doodads-kit/v1/farmyard.py -- grain-sacks` (only after removing this source), then `scripts/world-art.sh build grain-sacks`.
- Dimensions in world units and comparison to player: about 0.95 m wide, 0.45 m deep and 0.7 m tall, about half the character's height. Voxels are 0.05 m.
- Ground pivot and forward direction: ground origin at the centre of the pile. The front of the pile faces -Y in Blender, which is +Z in Godot.
- Visible overhang versus solid logical footprint: the proposed footprint is the pile, `[-0.475, -0.225, 0.95, 0.45]`. Nothing overhangs it.
- Shared materials, textures and import requirements: flat colours only. Burlap (`burlap`, `burlap_light`, `burlap_dark`) and twine (`twine`) are new to this kit and are defined in `farmyard.py`, not in `art/world_palette.gd`.
- Variant names and parts that move or change state: none. Objects are grouped by the `part` property: `Sacks` and `Twine`.
- Fields/commands exposed to player programs, or decorative only: decorative only.
- Active runtime output and catalog entry: `assets/world/grain-sacks/model.glb` and its `manifest.json`; catalog id `grain-sacks`, authority `blender`, status `candidate_unplaced`.
- Build command and second-placement/rebuild check: `scripts/world-art.sh build grain-sacks`. Placed through build mode in `test/test_build_mode.gd` and `tools/doodad_tour.gd`, which also exercise move, delete and save.
- Acceptance review/evidence and known limitations: at gameplay zoom (camera ortho 13) the pile still reads as a lumpy stone cluster rather than tied sacks. The two lower sacks merge into one mass, and the top sack's tie is too small to read. This needs a deliberate redesign, not more tweaks to the same profile.
