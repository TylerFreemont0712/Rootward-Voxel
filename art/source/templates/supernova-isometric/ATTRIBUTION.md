# Isometric animation pose reference

**Creator:** Supernova Files. **License:** CC0 1.0.

Downloaded 2026-10-23 from https://supernovafiles.itch.io/isometric-asset-pack through its normal free download flow. The creator explicitly marks the pack CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/.

`original.zip` is the untouched download. Walk and idle PNG/Aseprite files are extracted unchanged. This is an authoring reference, not the selected runtime character.

The walk PNG has twelve 64×64 frames in each of eight rows. The Aseprite layer names and cel positions establish the row order: S, SE, SW, W, NW, N, NE, E. The idle PNG has eight frames per row. Do not assume compass order.

`*-guide.png` arrange walk indices 0, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11 plus idle index 0 into a 3×3 sheet. They are enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling and numbered for pose correspondence. The guides supply motion only; the original painted adventurer supplies identity, proportions, palette and style.
